Control tips
MMB pans · wheel zooms · RMB creates a character.
Click a character to select them. Drag the card to move it freely.
Ctrl + click another character to marry them. Repeat it to unmarry them.
Shift + click a parent after selecting the child. Repeat it to remove that parent.
F/M circle changes gender when the character is not married or a parent.
Small symbol circle changes sexuality. Shift-click cycles backward.
Birth box edits the birth date. The pencil opens Extra Info.
Delete removes the selected character. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo.
